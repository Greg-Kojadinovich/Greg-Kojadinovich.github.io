What It Does

PleinColorWheel is a plein air painting learning tool engineered around subtractive RYB pigment physics — the color system that governs every stroke on a real canvas. Hold it up to the landscape, identify your hues, and know exactly what to mix before your brush ever touches paint.

The Differentiator

Most apps ignore what happens when you switch from oil to watercolor. PleinColorWheel doesn't — because your paint does. The mixing engine recalculates for each medium's real pigment behavior.

Feature 01

Subtractive RYB Physics

Not additive RGB. Real pigment mixing math — Red, Yellow, Blue — the system your paint actually obeys on canvas.

Feature 02

Medium-Specific Presets

Oil, Acrylic, and Watercolor each behave differently. The model adjusts to how your paint actually flows and blends.

Feature 03

Harmony Engine

Complementary, split-comp, triadic, analogous — reads your hues and surfaces actionable color relationships instantly.

Feature 04

Named Pigment Library

Match landscape hues to real pigment names so your field observations connect directly to the tubes in your kit.
